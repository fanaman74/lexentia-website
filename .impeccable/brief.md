# Lexentia visual replacement

Mode: Persuade on firm pages; Read on insight and legal pages.

Reference: EU JUST, visually reviewed in Chromium. User explicitly requested its UI/UX style.

First viewport: a compact pale header, lime-tile Lexentia wordmark, large upright Fraunces headline and plain-language introduction at left, and a pale-green illustration of Brussels’ Palace of Justice at right. On mobile, the headline leads and the landmark sits beneath it. The hero includes a slow architectural camera move and sparse wind-blown leaves with a pause control. Two clear expertise/contact actions preserve the firm site's purpose.

Shared grammar: pale #fcfff7 canvas, Geist body type, Fraunces headings, lime calls to action, hairline borders, 12px panels, muted green secondary text, dark violet-black editorial passages, restrained pink/mint practice panels. Reference offset shadows are retained on primary buttons and the homepage feed. No grid backdrop or italic green hero.

Interaction: quiet arrow shifts and active navigation; native disclosure rows for client situations; immediate article category/search/sort filtering with a visible result count and reset state. The analysis section reproduces EU JUST’s 30-second flowing wave, three-second scan, staggered card reveals/highlights, pause and replay controls. Decorative loops pause offscreen and in hidden tabs; reduced-motion users see static artwork and fully visible cards. Content remains accessible without animation.

Scope: local repository redesign; no deployment target is configured or requested. All translated routes, firm facts, legal draft notices, and source links remain available. Known contact-delivery and article-migration limitations remain explicit.

Homepage order: hero, Bienvenue/Welcome, Notre équipe/Our team (Nathalie’s official portrait; Magali placeholder), then two Matières préférentielles/practice cards. Existing research and firm content follows without duplicated team/practice sections.
