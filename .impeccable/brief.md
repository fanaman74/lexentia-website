# Lexentia visual replacement

Mode: Persuade on firm pages; Read on insight and legal pages.

Reference: EU JUST, visually reviewed in Chromium. User explicitly requested its UI/UX style.

First viewport: a compact pale header, supplied teal/purple Lexentia wordmark with an animated square pattern above the i, large upright Fraunces headline and plain-language introduction at left, and a pale-green illustration of Brussels’ Palace of Justice at right. On mobile, the headline leads and the landmark sits beneath it. The hero includes a slow architectural camera move and sparse wind-blown leaves with flying birds and a pause control. Two clear expertise/contact actions preserve the firm site's purpose.

Shared grammar: pale #fafcfb canvas, Geist body type, Fraunces headings, teal calls to action, hairline borders, 12px panels, muted green secondary text, dark violet-black editorial passages, compact square lavender/mint practice cards with a dedicated architectural image above compact text. Reference offset shadows are retained on primary buttons. No grid backdrop or italic green hero.

Interaction: quiet arrow shifts and active navigation; native disclosure rows for client situations; immediate article category/search/sort filtering with a visible result count and reset state. The homepage Latest News block follows the supplied WhatsApp carousel layout: title and circular navigation at left, one large white card at right, with a 900ms eased horizontal slide, mouse/touch swipe, previous/next controls and a seamless forward loop every 6.5 seconds. Autoplay pauses on hover/focus, offscreen, in hidden tabs and for reduced motion; a pause control is available. Desktop cards travel across the entire left text column, with a spatial mask and a slower 900ms linear exit fade; incoming cards fade in over 650ms. It shows the five latest court records in Latest News, with departing cards fading as they slide left and incoming cards fading into view. Decorative loops pause offscreen and in hidden tabs; reduced-motion users see static artwork and immediate carousel changes. Content remains accessible without animation.

Scope: local repository redesign; no deployment target is configured or requested. All translated routes, firm facts, legal draft notices, and source links remain available. Known contact-delivery and article-migration limitations remain explicit.

Homepage order: hero, Bienvenue/Welcome, Notre équipe/Our team (Nathalie’s official portrait; Magali placeholder), then two Matières préférentielles/practice cards. Existing research and firm content follows without duplicated team/practice sections.

User update: use the supplied logo palette throughout: teal primary actions/navigation/focus, purple secondary accents and private-law panels, green and amber sparingly. Regular sections reveal once on scroll; the homepage carousel replaces the previously requested analysis scan.

Filters cover court, matched topic and keyword, with decision-date/name sorting and 20 rows per page. Court records live on Latest News; firm-authored analyses remain on their own Insights page. Each local case page clearly labels decision date, source, import date and matched-topic limitations. No automatic monitoring claim and no attributed lawyer author for imported court records.
