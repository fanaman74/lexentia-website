# Lexentia visual replacement

Mode: Persuade on firm pages; Read on insight and legal pages.

Reference: EU JUST, visually reviewed in Chromium. User explicitly requested its UI/UX style.

First viewport: a compact pale header, supplied teal/purple Lexentia wordmark with an animated square pattern above the i, large upright Fraunces headline and plain-language introduction at left, and a pale-green illustration of Brussels’ Palace of Justice at right. On mobile, the headline leads and the landmark sits beneath it. The hero includes a slow architectural camera move and sparse wind-blown leaves with a pause control. Two clear expertise/contact actions preserve the firm site's purpose.

Shared grammar: pale #fafcfb canvas, Geist body type, Fraunces headings, teal calls to action, hairline borders, 12px panels, muted green secondary text, dark violet-black editorial passages, restrained lavender/mint practice panels with fine-line background illustrations. Reference offset shadows are retained on primary buttons and the homepage feed. No grid backdrop or italic green hero.

Interaction: quiet arrow shifts and active navigation; native disclosure rows for client situations; immediate article category/search/sort filtering with a visible result count and reset state. The homepage Insights block now follows the supplied WhatsApp carousel layout: title and circular navigation at left, one large white card at right, with a 750ms eased horizontal slide, mouse/touch swipe and bounded previous/next controls. It shows the five latest Insights, with departing cards fading as they slide left and incoming cards fading into view. Decorative loops pause offscreen and in hidden tabs; reduced-motion users see static artwork and immediate carousel changes. Content remains accessible without animation.

Scope: local repository redesign; no deployment target is configured or requested. All translated routes, firm facts, legal draft notices, and source links remain available. Known contact-delivery and article-migration limitations remain explicit.

Homepage order: hero, Bienvenue/Welcome, Notre équipe/Our team (Nathalie’s official portrait; Magali placeholder), then two Matières préférentielles/practice cards. Existing research and firm content follows without duplicated team/practice sections.

User update: use the supplied logo palette throughout: teal primary actions/navigation/focus, purple secondary accents and private-law panels, green and amber sparingly. Regular sections reveal once on scroll; the homepage carousel replaces the previously requested analysis scan.

Insights extension: inherit EU JUST’s date/title/court-reference row structure for 84 real court records, preserving English official titles and EUR-Lex links. Filters cover court, matched topic and keyword, with decision-date/name sorting and 20 rows per page. Court records lead; firm-authored analyses remain in a separate warm section below. Each local case page clearly labels decision date, source, import date and matched-topic limitations. No automatic monitoring claim and no attributed lawyer author for imported court records.

WhatsApp reference: user-supplied download-page screenshot. Live Chromium inspection was attempted but www.whatsapp.com is outside the cloud network allowlist, so exact live easing was not verified. The full searchable court archive remains on the Insights page, as confirmed by the user.
